About the show

Meet Jessica & the show

Jessica Johnson started The Upside Down Influencer to ask a simple question out loud: what would it look like if our social media actually looked like our faith?

Each episode pairs honest conversation with practical next steps for the Christian 20- and 30-something who scrolls, posts, and wonders sometimes if any of it means anything. The show's answer: it can, if we let it be set apart.

"Set apart over popular."

What the show is about

Four threads run through every episode: discernment in a culture of comparison, courage to post (or not post) as an act of faith, community over clout, and practical rhythms for a healthier relationship with the scroll.
